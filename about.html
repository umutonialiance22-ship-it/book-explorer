About the project

Welcome to Enchanted Reads

Enchanted Reads is a book discovery web application designed for readers who love fantasy, romantic fantasy, magical adventures, mythical creatures, and unforgettable characters.

The goal is to make discovering your next favorite book enjoyable through a simple, responsive, and interactive browsing experience. Readers can search for books, explore categories, and save their favorites for later.

Discover

Explore fantasy adventures, romantic stories, magical kingdoms, and new worlds through a searchable book collection.

Save Your Favorites

Keep track of books you want to read using a favorites collection stored in your browser.

Built with Web Technologies

This project brings together HTML, Tailwind CSS, JavaScript, ES modules, browser storage, and API integration.

Our Mission

Make it easier for fantasy and romance readers to find stories that capture their imagination.
